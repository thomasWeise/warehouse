warehouse.event package¶

The possible events that can be encountered during the simulation.

These include

  • events related to the simulation time, such as StartEvent and EndEvent,

  • events related to storing parts into a warehouse or retrieving them, as implemented in storage, as well as

  • events related to storing parts into bins of a given type inside

    warehouse or retrieving them from such as bins, as implemented in bins.
