warehouse.simulation package¶

The warehouse simulator.

This package contains a simulator of warehouse activities. All the modules and classes except for implemented in module Simulation in module simulation should be considered as package-private. They realize different modules/parts of the simulation.

warehouse.simulation.module_storage module¶

The warehouse storage simulation.

In this class, we implement the functionality needed by the local warehouse. This only concerns the movement of part units inside the warehouse, the putting of units of part into the warehouse, and the taking of units of part out of the warehouse. This is completely separate from any other logic, such as the re-ordering of part, the consumption of part at the assembly line, or anything else.
